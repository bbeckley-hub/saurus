SCCmec Typing

Staphylococcal Cassette Chromosome mec
How to read this page
  • SCCmec is the staphylococcal cassette chromosome carrying the methicillin-resistance gene mecA/mecC. It is classified into types I–XIII by the combination of its mec complex and ccr gene complex; subtypes are lettered (IIA, IIB, …).
  • CGE tab — types assigned by CGE SCCmecFinder, with its Confidence and Evidence tags. Missing/uncalled types are shown as Not Assigned.
  • RPet tab — types assigned with Robert Petit III's SCCmec scheme (shortened RPet here), called through StaphScope. It reports the type, subtype, mec class, ccr complexes and the SCCmec genes present.
  • Unified tab merges both callers per genome: MRSA status prefers RPet and falls back to CGE, and Agreement compares the two roman-numeral types — Agree, Disagree, One Missing or Both Missing.
  • Use the ⚙ Columns menu to reveal hidden fields — including the 14 NCBI assembly/QC columns (contigs, N50, GC %, CheckM, platform, …) — and Show all/Reset on each tab. Type, subtype and source boxes are searchable; the continent is derived from the sampling country.
