📖 About this data

All metadata comes from NCBI BioSample records attached to the RefSeq and GenBank assemblies. Country, isolation source, host, and collection year are extracted from the standard BioSample attributes. Sources have been normalized into broad categories (blood, nasal/screening, skin/wound, food, respiratory, …); raw values are preserved in the TSV/CSV/JSON exports as country_raw and source_raw. Sequencing platforms are likewise normalized (ILLUMINA → Illumina), with the verbatim submission kept in the sequencing_tech_raw export column.

The new update added 14 assembly/QC fields — genome length, chromosome/contig/scaffold counts, N50 values, GC %, WGS accession, normalized and raw sequencing platform, CheckM completeness/contamination, and the NCBI strain. They are hidden by default in the table; open ⚙ Columns to show any of them, and they are all included in the exports. CheckM values cover only the genomes in NCBI's quality reports (about a fifth of the collection). Blank cells are shown as — — the underlying data is genuinely missing, not filtered out. The SRA coverage figure reflects NCBI SRA deposition: 16.6% of genomes have publicly available raw reads. Use the SRA Run column in the export to feed fastq-dl and the SRA Toolkit.
